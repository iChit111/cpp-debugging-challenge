1. When both Employee and Student inherited from Person,
   it created two copies of everything present in the Person class.
   Thus, 2 objects were created in the original inheritance structure.

2. The diamond inheritance problem occurs when the compiler throws
   an ambiguity error because it doesn't know from which class it is supposed
   to inherit the grandparent class's members. It specifically occurs when
   hierarchial and multiple inheritance coexist in the program.

3. The 'virtual' keyword ensures that the members of the parent class are shared
   and thus only a single copy is created.

4. Adding the 'virtual' keyword is required in both Student and Employee class.
